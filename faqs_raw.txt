Here are formal responses FAQ questions for the NSS Teaching and Technical Wing of IIT Patna:
1. What is NSS?
 The National Service Scheme (NSS) is a central-sector public service program under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India. It aims to develop the personality and character of student volunteers through community service. NSS provides students with the opportunity to engage in various social initiatives, enabling them to contribute meaningfully to society while gaining essential life skills.
________________


2. What is the significance of NSS?
 NSS plays a vital role in instilling a sense of social responsibility, discipline, and empathy among students. Through activities such as teaching underprivileged children, organizing technical workshops, and participating in community outreach programs, students develop leadership qualities, problem-solving skills, and a commitment to nation-building. NSS bridges the gap between academic learning and real-world challenges, fostering holistic development.
________________


3. Where is NSS conducted?
 NSS is implemented across numerous educational institutions in India, including all major IITs, NITs, and central universities. At IIT Patna, NSS functions through dedicated wings such as Teaching, Technical, and Creative, each focusing on specific areas of service. Similar structures and objectives are observed in other IITs, promoting a shared spirit of community engagement across campuses.
________________


4. How many hours are required to complete NSS?
 To fulfill the NSS requirement during the undergraduate program, a student must complete a minimum of 120 hours of community service in the 1st year of your graduation. These hours include both regular activities and special camps, with consistent participation being essential for successful completion and certification.
________________


5. How is NSS different from NSO and NCC?
 NSS (National Service Scheme), NSO (National Sports Organization), and NCC (National Cadet Corps) are all avenues for co-curricular engagement offered to students, each with a unique focus:
* NSS centers around community service and social responsibility. It allows students to apply their knowledge and skills to improve societal conditions, especially through education, awareness, and technical support.

* NSO focuses on physical fitness and skill development in various sports, promoting health and team spirit.

* NCC provides structured military training and instills discipline, leadership, and patriotism.

Among these, NSS stands out as a platform for social impact, enabling students to directly contribute to the betterment of communities through practical engagement, especially in areas like teaching and technology. It offers flexibility, diversity in activities, and a hands-on approach to societal development, making it a fulfilling and enriching experience.

TEACHING AND TECHNICAL WING, NSS IIT PATNA


The Teaching and Technical Wing operates through three sub-divisions, each responsible for both specific and general tasks related to the wing. All three sub-divisions working in unison contribute to the overall functioning of the wing. Each sub-division is supported by dedicated mentors who guide volunteers in their respective domains.


GENERAL QUESTIONS :


Q1. What are the various sub-divisions existing in the Teaching Wing and what are their functions?
Ans : There are a total of three sub-divisions existing in the Teaching Wing –
   1. Core teaching Committee
   2. Events Committee
   3. Social Media & Photography Committee


Core teaching Committee : Responsible for all activities related to teaching. Teaching activities can range from teaching in government schools, conducting events in various schools, technical teaching in schools and tutorial blocks to even online teaching.


Events Committee :  All the events that happen in the institution related to social activity are managed by the Events Committee. It includes Event management, Logistics, seating arrangement, event decision and execution.


Social Media & Photography Committee : Graphic designing for posts and video editing for reels for the official social media The Teaching and Technical Wing. Also responsible for handling the photography and videography at the events.


Q2. How can I get selected for a particular committee in the Teaching and Technical Wing ?


Ans : Volunteers for a particular committee will be selected based on their interview score and skill set by the sub-coordinators and co-ordinators of the wing. However, volunteers can inform about their particular preference if any during the time of the interview.


Q3. If I am initially allotted to a particular committee after the interview, will I have an opportunity to change it later?


Ans : The initial committee allotted might change upon vacancies and as per your interest but there is no guarantee to it, moreover the initial allotment will be done on the basis of interests.
Q4. If I am part of the Events or Social Media & Photography Committee, will I still have the opportunity to teach students?


Ans : Teaching students is the most touching and fulfilling part of being in the Teaching and Technical Wing. We believe every volunteer deserves to experience it, so no matter which committee you're in, you'll have the opportunity to take part in teaching activities.


Q5. How many hours will I obtain from the Teaching Wing ?  How many hours am I required to complete in the first and second semesters?


Ans : To be decided


Q6. What will happen if I am unable to complete the required number of hours ? Can I complete my remaining hours from other NSS activities ?


Ans : If you are unable to complete the required number of hours in the Teaching and Technical Wing, you will not be considered to have passed NSS. You must complete 80 hours (or the final decided number) over the course of two semesters.


Q7.Can I choose to leave the Teaching and Technical Wing at the end of the first semester?


Ans : Leaving the Teaching and Technical Wing at the end of the first semester is allowed, but only if you have completed 40 hours. If your total is less than 40 hours and you still wish to leave, you must complete the remaining hours in the second semester through teaching activities, even if you are no longer part of the Teaching Wing.


Q8. Will there be a system in place for volunteers to monitor their hours throughout the semester?


Ans : Volunteers will be able to view the points they have earned through the Teaching and Technical Wing once or twice every month. Final hours will be displayed only after the semester ends.


Q9. If I am unable to attend a class or event due to illness or a personal reason, whom should I inform ? Will I have an opportunity to make up for my absence?


Ans : If a volunteer is unable to attend a class or event due to illness or a personal reason, they must inform the Teaching and Technical Wing in advance by sending an email to <hyperlink containing email address>.
All volunteers will have sufficient opportunities throughout the semester to make up for any absences. It is the responsibility of the wing to ensure that such opportunities are provided.


Q10. Is it compulsory to buy the Teaching and Technical Wing T-shirt? Am I required to wear it at all times?


Ans : Yes, it is mandatory for every volunteer to purchase the T-shirt, as it is compulsory for volunteers from all NSS wings to buy their respective T-shirts. Wearing the T-shirt will be required while taking classes or attending events.


Q11. Will there be a transportation facility available for volunteers? Will NSS provide compensation for transportation costs?


Ans : Transportation facilities will not be available for volunteers. Since the schools are located nearby, volunteers are encouraged to use their own bicycles.Please note that NSS will not reimburse any transportation expenses.


Q12. In case of an emergency or any complaints/questions, whom should I contact ?


Ans : 
In case of an emergency or if you have any questions, you should first contact the concerned POC.
If you do not receive a response, you may reach out to any sub-coordinator of your committee.
If the issue remains unresolved, you can then contact the head sub-coordinator.
Finally, if you still have any grievances or complaints, feel free to contact the coordinators.
Remember, we are always here to support and help you!

COMMITTEE SPECIFIC QUESTIONS :


Events Committee :
1. What happens in the Events Committee?
Answer: The Events Committee is responsible for managing all college events related to social activities organized by the Teaching Wing. This includes event planning and execution, logistics, seating arrangements, and overall event management.


2. What will be the role of a Teaching and Technical Wing volunteer managing an event?
Answer: A volunteer managing an event will be responsible for ensuring its smooth execution. Their duties may include event documentation, managing logistics, arranging seating, handling crowd control, and assisting with event planning and execution.




 3. What will we learn by becoming a volunteer in the Events Committee?
Answer: As a volunteer in the Events Committee, you will develop essential skills such as event management, effective communication, teamwork, planning and on-ground execution, as well as official documentation practices.


4. Will the event volunteer have to do teaching too?
Ans : Yes, but the teaching load will be reduced in order to ensure fair workload.


Q5: In case of a conflict of suggestions among volunteers, who will make the final decision?
Answer: In the event of creative conflicts, the decision of the sub-coordinators will be final. Their decision will be well-justified and taken in the best interest of the event and the team.


Q6: How many events will be held in a month?
Answer: As of now, there will be a minimum of 2 and a maximum of 4 events each month. However, the number may vary depending on specific requirements or circumstances.


7. Is there anything other than the mentioned?
Ans : Yes, there are some school events planned, which require supervision and will be monitored directly under the subcoordinators.


Q8: Will each volunteer be required to attend all the events?
Answer: No, each volunteer will attend a designated number of events that ensures fairness and matches the workload of non-event volunteers. The exact threshold will be specified in the official document.


Q9: Why is there a separate Events Committee?
Answer: The Events Committee exists to support the social service mission of the Teaching and Technical Wing by engaging a larger, more active audience. Since events are a key way to reach people, the committee operates independently to plan and execute impactful and successful events.




Social Media & Photography Committee :


1. What activities happen within the Social Media and Photography committee?
Ans: Graphic designing, Video editing, Photography and videography at the events. May include a little acting.


2. How much work will a volunteer have to do?
Ans: A single volunteer will be assigned a maximum of 1 task per week. In very extreme situations, this may go to 2 tasks per week.


3. On which platform will the volunteer do graphic design?
Ans: Most of the time, we will use Canva and Figma since these provide collaborative work.


4. Will the committee's volunteers also have to do teaching ?
Ans: Yes. But some relaxations will be there to ensure that any volunteer is not being overloaded.


5. When will the volunteers do photography and videography?
Ans: Photography and videography are majorly done at the various events organised by the wing. May also be done at interviews or promotional skits.


6. What to do if the volunteers have doubts regarding their content/design/photos?
Ans: The decision of the sub-coordinators will be final on the quality of the content created by the volunteer. Hence the volunteer will be suggested changes in case the creation is not up to the mark.

FAQ’s for School Teaching :


Q1. In how many schools do we provide full fledged regular school teaching ?

A: Currently we provide 3 schools with regular full fledged school teaching : Raghopur , Amhara School & TPS School. All of the above 3 are government schools. 


Q2: When will I have to go to teach in these schools?
Answer: The teaching schedule will be shared with you at the beginning of each week or month. It will be planned in alignment with your academic timetable, so you don’t need to worry about any clashes.


Q3: What subject will I be teaching in these schools?
Answer: You may be assigned different subjects based on the school's requirements. The subject you are expected to teach will be clearly mentioned in the teaching schedule provided to you.


Q. What topic will I be teaching on my assigned day?


A:  All topics will be listed in a properly planned chronological order in your course documents. An Excel sheet will be maintained where you can check the last topic covered by the previous volunteer. You are expected to continue from the next topic in the sequence.


Q4: What should I do if I arrive at the school and find that it’s a holiday or no students have shown up and the classroom is empty ?
A: This is a very common situation you might encounter during your volunteering journey. We will do our best to inform you in advance if there is a holiday. However, if you still arrive and find the school closed or the class empty, you may take a photograph as proof and return to your hostel. While you won’t receive attendance points for that day since the class wasn't conducted, you will be given an opportunity later to make up for it.


Q5. If a teacher refuses to let me enter the class, what should I do ?


A: Do not panic or argue with the teacher. If possible, try to contact the concerned POC for assistance. If you're unable to reach them, you can return back to the hostel.


Q6. If the students refuse to study and leave the class, what should I do ?


A:  You may kindly request the students to return to the classroom. If a teacher is nearby, you can seek their assistance in managing the situation. Otherwise, you can proceed with teaching the students who are present and interested.


Q7. If a particular group of students are creating a nuisance, what should I do ?
A: Try to implement innovative and engaging methods to capture the students’ interest. If a particular student or two is being disruptive, you may politely ask them to step out of the classroom. However, please ensure that you remain calm and composed — avoid shouting or any form of aggressive behavior.


Q8. If I go there and find that the topic I have prepared has already been covered, what should I do ?


A: You can teach the next topic if it is an easy one and you have prior knowledge. Otherwise, you can discuss question answers or summarize the entire topic once. 


Q9. Do I need to carry some items for teaching purposes ?


A: In general, not required but if you feel you need any teaching tool to explain a particular topic, you might carry one.  For technical teaching sessions, carrying your personal laptop is mandatory to ensure proper delivery of the content.

Volunteers play a crucial role in creating a safe and productive environment for all learners.
