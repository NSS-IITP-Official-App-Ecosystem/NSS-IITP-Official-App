# Custom Agent Rules
*This file contains instructions that the AI assistant MUST follow during all interactions for this project.*

1. **Do not build or compile the app for me.** I will build the app myself in Android Studio and report back any errors or success.
2. **Keep explanations non-technical and student-friendly.** When creating walkthroughs or explaining features, tell me exactly what to click, where to look in the app, and what specific fields to check in the Firestore database (avoiding code-level jargon).
3. **Be short and concise.** Provide information directly without making things too lengthy or overwhelming to read.
